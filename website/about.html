About

Don't miss the best of the city.

Thessaloniki has 2,300 years of history packed into a walkable city, yet most visitors leave having seen the White Tower and little else.

TreasureThess puts the places worth seeing on one map, from Roman arches and Byzantine churches to the upper town walls and the old markets, each with a short story in English and Greek. Check in when you get there and the city keeps a record of your trip.

It's built to be a game. Every place earns points, collections reward you for seeing a whole side of the city, and badges, friends and leaderboards keep it fun. At the end, your Thessaloniki card sums up everywhere you've been, ready to share.

The points stay local. Partner cafés, shops and museums offer coupons that you unlock with what you earn: more walking, more discovery, and more reasons to visit the city's small businesses.
