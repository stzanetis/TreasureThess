Delete your account

In the app (instant)

  1. Open TreasureThess and go to the Me tab.
  2. Tap the settings icon in the top right.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Without the app

Email hello@treasurethess.app from the address you signed up with, and we will delete your account within 30 days.

What gets deleted

Your profile, email, photo, points, check-ins, follows, reviews and coupons are deleted permanently.
