Privacy policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

This policy explains what the TreasureThess app and website collect, why, and what choices you have. Contact: hello@treasurethess.app.

What we collect

  • Account: your email address, password (stored hashed by our auth provider), display name, and optionally a profile photo and bio.
  • Activity: the places you check in to and when, points you earn and spend, coupons you redeem, people you follow, and reviews you post.
  • Location: used only while the app is open, to show places near you. When you check in, your position is sent once to confirm you are at the place, and it is not stored. If you tap a TreasureThess tag, only the tag's ID is sent.
  • Photos you choose to upload for your profile.
  • Partner applications submitted on this website: business and contact details you enter.

What is public

Your display name, profile photo, bio, total points, the places you visited, your reviews and your followers are visible to other users. Your email, your location and your coupon codes are never shown to others.

Why we use it

To run the service: your account, check-ins, points, leaderboards, the community feed and rewards. We may share anonymous, aggregated statistics (for example, how many people visited a place) with partners. We do not sell your personal data or use it for advertising.

Who processes it

  • Supabase hosts our database, authentication and image storage in the EU (Ireland).
  • CARTO and OpenStreetMap provide the map tiles. Your device requests tiles directly, so these services see your IP address.
  • Expo builds and distributes the app.

How long we keep it

Until you delete your account.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can access, correct, export or delete your data, and object to its processing. You can edit your profile and delete your account inside the app at any time. For anything else, email us. You can also complain to the Hellenic Data Protection Authority (dpa.gr).

Children

TreasureThess is not intended for children under 13.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
