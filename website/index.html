How it works

Explore, check in, get rewarded.

FAQ

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. The app is free and so are the points. Some museums charge for entry as usual.

How does checking in work?

Open the place, or tap the big button in the middle of the app, when you're within about 100 m. Your phone's location confirms you're there. Some places also have a TreasureThess tag you can tap with an NFC phone.

Do you track my location?

Only while the app is open, to show places near you. When you check in, your position is sent once to confirm you're at the place and is not stored. We only save which place you visited and when.

Can I use it in Greek?

Yes. The app follows your phone's language and you can switch between English and Ελληνικά in Settings.

I run a shop, café or museum. Can I join?

Yes, see the partner program.
