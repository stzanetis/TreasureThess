Partner program

Turn explorers into customers.

TreasureThess users walk from sight to sight, many of them visitors who don't know the city yet. Offer them a coupon and give them a reason to stop at your door.

Apply to join

Why partner with us

  • Your business appears in the app's Rewards tab, in English and Greek
  • Visitors earn the coupon by exploring, so they arrive motivated to use it
  • You choose the offer, the stock and the expiry date
  • Museums and attractions can also become places to check in to
How redemption works

Simple at the counter.

1

Visitor redeems

They spend points on your coupon in the app. It waits in their wallet.

2

Shows it live

At your counter they tap "Show to staff". A code, a QR and a 10-minute countdown appear.

3

You check the timer

A moving "LIVE" badge and a running timer mean it's real, not a screenshot. Once the timer ends, the coupon is used.
